Privacy Policy for Pokémon Solitaire

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

Pokémon Solitaire collects the following information through the Reddit platform:

  • Username: Your Reddit username (used solely for game progress identification).
  • Game Progress: Data such as floor level, experience points, gold, and achievements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To save your game progress and stats across different sessions.
  • To enable cross-device synchronization of your game state.
  • To display your username within the game interface.

3. Data Storage

All game data is stored securely on Reddit's servers using their native storage solutions (Redis). We do not maintain external servers, and no personal data is shared with third parties.

4. Data Deletion

If you wish to delete your game data, please contact the developer via Reddit direct message or delete the app from your Reddit account settings (if applicable).

5. Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via Reddit direct messages.
