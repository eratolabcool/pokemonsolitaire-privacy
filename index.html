Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

Pokémon Solitaire collects the following information through Reddit:

  • Username: Your Reddit username (for game progress identification)
  • Game Progress: Floor level, experience points, gold, achievements (stored in Reddit Redis)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

  • Save your game progress across sessions
  • Enable cross-device synchronization
  • Display your username in the game

3. Data Storage

All game data is stored securely on Reddit's servers using Redis. No personal data is shared with third parties.

4. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your game data, please contact the developer through Reddit.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact through Reddit direct messages.
